About Srishtipadam

Srishtipadam is a premier literary community and publication organization based in Kerala, India. Founded with a vision to nurture creative minds, we bridge the gap between aspiring writers and readers. Through our extensive district-wise network and publication initiatives, we strive to enrich the Malayalam literary landscape and preserve our rich cultural heritage for future generations.

Our Vision

To establish a vibrant, inclusive, and globally recognized platform for Malayalam literature, empowering every creative voice to express, publish, and inspire without boundaries.

Our Mission

To discover talent at the grassroots level, publish high-quality literary works, organize engaging literary events across all Kerala districts, and build a supportive ecosystem for writers.
